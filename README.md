# Manufacturing Estimating Platform

An engineering-focused platform for moving manufacturing requests for quotation from intake to reviewed customer quote.

## What the product is

The product is being designed for machine shops, CNC manufacturers, fabricators, welding operations, sheet-metal businesses, and other manufacturers that need a more consistent estimating workflow.

Its intended capabilities include:

- Centralized RFQ and drawing intake
- Assisted review of manufacturing documents
- Traceable, itemized cost estimates
- Editable manufacturing routes and assumptions
- Quantity-tier pricing and lead-time support
- Mandatory human review and approval
- Customer-ready quotation generation
- Estimate-versus-actual reporting

AI assists with document interpretation and proposed requirements. Auditable software performs pricing calculations, and authorized people retain control over approval and quote release.

## Project status

This project is in active development. Features and availability may change as the platform is validated with real manufacturing workflows.
